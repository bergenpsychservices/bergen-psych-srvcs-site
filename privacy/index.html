Website privacy

Your information deserves care

This notice explains how information is handled when you use this website or request a consultation.

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Information you choose to provide

The consultation form asks for your name, email address, and preferred contact method. A phone number is required when you choose phone contact; a preferred contact time is optional. These details are used to respond to your inquiry and arrange a consultation.

How your request is delivered

Form submissions are processed through StaticForms and delivered to the practice’s email inbox. Information is therefore handled by the form service and email provider as part of delivering and responding to your request. The practice does not sell your consultation information or use it for marketing.

Please keep health information out of the form

The website form and ordinary email are intended for initial contact and scheduling. They are not a confidential clinical messaging service. Please do not submit clinical histories, diagnoses, insurance numbers, payment information, or other sensitive details.

We can discuss your concerns during a consultation and review appropriate ways to communicate about care. Sending a request does not establish a therapist–client relationship.

Website visits

This website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand aggregate website traffic and page performance. The analytics script loads from Cloudflare and sends visit and performance measurements to Cloudflare. Consultation form entries are not sent to analytics. This website does not use advertising trackers.

Fonts and practice photographs are hosted with the site. The Psychology Today verified seal loads from Psychology Today, so your browser connects to its service when displaying the seal. The website host and service providers may process technical information, such as IP addresses and request logs, to deliver and operate their services.

Questions or deletion requests

To ask about information you submitted or request its deletion, contact Bergen Psychology Services at (201) 366-2442 or alan@bergenpsychologyservices.com. Requests will be considered in light of applicable recordkeeping requirements.

Clinical privacy

This website notice concerns website use and consultation inquiries. Information about clinical confidentiality and the practice’s handling of treatment records is provided as part of starting care.
