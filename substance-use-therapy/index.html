Substance use & behavior change

Substance use therapy in Bergen County, New Jersey

You may be questioning your relationship with alcohol or other substances, concerned about a pattern, or ready to make a change. We can start with where you are.

Understanding substance use and its effects

Alcohol or other substances may provide short-term relief from stress or difficult emotions while contributing to longer-term problems in relationships, work, or well-being. Exploring both sides of that experience helps us understand what maintains a pattern and what changes may matter to you.

You do not need to arrive with a diagnosis or a fully settled goal. We can begin by talking openly about your concerns, motivations, and priorities.

When substance use and mental health overlap

Substance use can be connected with anxiety and depression or with trauma-related experiences. Understanding how these concerns interact is often an important part of planning treatment.

My clinical work and research have focused on behavioral interventions for co-occurring mental health and substance use concerns. I bring that perspective to collaborative care that considers your individual circumstances and goals.

Developing a practical plan for change

We may identify situations that influence use, explore triggers and urges, and develop alternative responses. Drawing on CBT and ACT, we'll choose strategies collaboratively and revisit progress and goals over time. Read about my therapeutic approach.

If you need medical evaluation or a more intensive level of support, we can discuss appropriate referrals. This practice does not provide medical detoxification.
