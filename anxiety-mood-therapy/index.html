Anxiety, stress & mood

Therapy for anxiety and depression in Bergen County

When worry consumes your attention or low mood makes everyday tasks harder, therapy can help you understand what is keeping these patterns going and consider ways forward.

How anxiety and depression affect daily life

Anxiety may involve persistent worry, second-guessing, feeling on edge, or avoiding uncertain situations. Depression can affect motivation, energy, concentration, and enjoyment, sometimes making routine responsibilities feel difficult.

Stress, changes in relationships, life transitions, and professional demands may contribute to these patterns. We'll explore both your symptoms and the broader context of your life.

What treatment may involve

Drawing primarily from Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) and Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT), we may explore thinking patterns, avoidance, or self-criticism, and practice new responses to difficult thoughts and emotions. Together we'll identify manageable ways to engage with activities, relationships, and goals that matter to you.

Therapy is individualized. We will revisit your goals, discuss what is helping, and adjust our work over time. Learn about my CBT and ACT approach.

Seeing the whole picture

Anxiety and depression may also overlap with trauma-related difficulties or substance use concerns. We can explore these connections rather than treating each challenge in isolation.
